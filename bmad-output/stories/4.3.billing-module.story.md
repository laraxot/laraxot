## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01 — Invoice CRUD linked to WorkOrder/Customer
Invoice model (number, issued_on, total, notes, electronic_invoice_sent_at, soft-deletes). BelongsTo Customer. InvoiceResource with List/Create/Edit/View pages. Extends XotBaseModel and XotBaseResource.

### AC-02 — InvoiceWorkOrderLink pivot
InvoiceWorkOrderLink pivot model (invoice_id, work_order_id). Unique constraint on pair. Invoice hasMany links; WorkOrder hasMany links. WorkOrdersRelationManager on InvoiceResource.

### AC-03 — DeliveryNote with lines
DeliveryNote model (received_on, soft-deletes). BelongsTo Supplier. HasMany DeliveryNoteLine (quantity, unit_price). DeliveryNoteResource with LinesRelationManager.

### AC-04 — Supplier registry
Supplier model (is_active, soft-deletes). HasMany DeliveryNote and SupplierInvoice. SupplierResource CRUD.

### AC-05 — SupplierInvoice (ciclo passivo)
SupplierInvoice model (issued_on, total, soft-deletes). BelongsTo Supplier. HasMany SupplierInvoiceLine (quantity, unit_price). SupplierInvoiceResource with LinesRelationManager. BuildPreInvoiceDraftAction and ReconcileSupplierInvoiceAction for matching DDT to supplier invoice.

### AC-06 — Electronic invoice tracking
Invoice model includes `electronic_invoice_sent_at` datetime. Integration with Fiscal module: GenerateFatturaElettronicaXmlAction called from InvoiceResource action.

### AC-07 — Flatten to Laraxot structure
Move models from `Infrastructure/Models/` to `app/Models/`, policies from `Infrastructure/Policies/` to `app/Policies/`, Filament resources to `app/Filament/Resources/`, actions from `Domain/Actions/` to `app/Actions/`, DTOs from `Domain/DTO/` to `app/DTO/`. Reconcile all namespace imports.

- **SRC reference:** `/var/www/_bases/gestionale_commesse/Modules/Billing/Domain/DTO/` — 4 DTOs (PreInvoiceDraft, PreInvoiceLine, ReconciliationDiscrepancy, SupplierLine).

- Invoice already has `electronic_invoice_sent_at` datetime column in SRC — must be carried forward.

- DeliveryNote is "documento di trasporto ricevuto dal fornitore" — inbound DDT.
- SupplierInvoice is "fattura ricevuta dal fornitore (ciclo passivo)" — reconciliation with DeliveryNote via ReconcileSupplierInvoiceAction.
- BuildPreInvoiceDraftAction generates a draft invoice from work order intervention lines.
- All models currently extend `Illuminate\Database\Eloquent\Model` directly; must migrate to `XotBaseModel`.
- Resources currently extend `Filament\Resources\Resource`; must migrate to `XotBaseResource`.
- **Migration strategy:** Flatten Infrastructure/Models/ → app/Models/, namespace changes from `Modules\Billing\Infrastructure\Models\` to `Modules\Billing\app\Models\`.
- Callers of Billing Fiscal integration pass through DTOs — Fiscal module is pure, no Eloquent access.
- Module already exists in base with DDD structure — flattening is the primary migration work.
