## Story

As an **agente AI / developer Laraxot**,
I want **`bashscripts/ai/.agents/skills` e `rules` sintetizzati, efficaci e recuperabili via second brain (QMD + TRIGGER_MAP)**,
so that **il preload token resti minimo e il lavoro sia agile senza perdere procedure**.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Ogni `SKILL.md` / `.mdc` non-HOT ha **≤40 righe** e punta a QMD + wiki (stub).
2. Body completo vive in `bashscripts/ai/.agents/archive/{skills,rules}/` (cold).
3. `hot-manifest.txt` elenca skill/rule critiche (bootstrap, second-brain, bmad router, module-config, …).
4. `audit-agents-on-demand-stubs.sh` → **OK** (gate ripetibile).
5. Router univoci: `skills/00-INDEX.md` → wiki skills INDEX; `rules/00-INDEX.md` → TRIGGER_MAP; `AGENTS.md` stub.
6. Skill operative BMAD usate da slash (`bmad-create-story`, ecc.) **non** restano stub inutili: HOT **o** stub che punta a SSoT wiki utilizzabile (`docs/wiki/skills/...`).
7. Canon wiki aggiornato: `agents-skills-rules-on-demand-compact.md` + riga TRIGGER_MAP + `on-demand-pattern.md`.
8. Agenti usano stack: healthcheck → TRIGGER 1 riga → `llm-wiki-qmd.sh search -n 5` → 1 skill — **mai** `ls` albero skills.
